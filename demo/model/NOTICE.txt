The two models the demo runs in the browser. Both licences are the Apache
License 2.0 (full text in ../lib/LICENSE-transformers.js.txt). The files are
unmodified copies; the SHA-256 of each model file matches the Hugging Face
repository.

all-MiniLM-L6-v2, a sentence-embedding model, matches players' questions to
Alexa's scripted answers.

Model:       sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2
             https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2
ONNX build:  Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (onnx/model_quantized.onnx)
             https://huggingface.co/Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2
SHA-256:     model_quantized.onnx
             afdb6f1a0e45b715d0bb9b11772f032c399babd23bfc31fed1c170afc848bdb1

whisper-tiny.en, a speech recognition model, turns what players say to Alexa
into text in browsers that can't do that themselves. It is only downloaded
the first time a player talks in one of those browsers.

Model:       openai/whisper-tiny.en
             https://huggingface.co/openai/whisper-tiny.en
ONNX build:  Xenova/whisper-tiny.en (onnx/encoder_model_quantized.onnx,
             onnx/decoder_model_merged_quantized.onnx)
             https://huggingface.co/Xenova/whisper-tiny.en
